| 23  | PROMPT-03 | **Invitation creation security & policy (binding):** Invitation tokens are generated from 256 bits of cryptographically secure randomness, encoded URL-safe (base64url), and persisted ONLY as a deterministic SHA-256 hex hash (`tokenHash`) — the raw token is returned from the creation operation exactly once, never persisted, never logged, and never included in errors/analytics; lookups are hash-only. Invitations expire 7 days after creation (`expiresAt = createdAt + TTL`, centralized as `INVITATION_EXPIRY_DAYS`). Duplicate OPEN invitations are prevented per Business + normalized (trimmed, lowercased) email at the workflow layer (transaction-scoped check-then-create in `InvitationRepository.createIfNoOpenInvitation`) — NOT via a DB uniqueness constraint (the same email may be invited by different Businesses and re-invited after expiry/revocation/acceptance; consistent with the Prompt-10 data-model decision). Expired, revoked, and accepted invitations never block a new invitation. Revocation is business-scoped, idempotent for already-revoked invitations, and forbidden for accepted ones.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | High-entropy random tokens with a deterministic hash give unguessable credentials with feasible lookup without storing the secret; a short explicit TTL bounds the window of an undelivered/stale invitation; per-Business duplicate-open prevention avoids confusing duplicate invites while keeping cross-Business and re-invitation semantics possible; transaction-scoped application logic mirrors the established appointment conflict-check pattern without new DB infrastructure (pilot volume makes the residual race acceptable, same as appointments). | Accepted |
